# Requirement audit — 2026-10-20

This audit follows the supplied native-Go rewrite plan. Completion means the implementation, deliverables and requested local checks are present and verified. Live console testing, live IGDB accounts, registry publication and execution of hosted CI are separate follow-ups; the plan requires honest reporting of those limits, not a claim that they occurred.
